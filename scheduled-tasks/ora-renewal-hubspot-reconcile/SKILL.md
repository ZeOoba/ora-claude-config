STEP 6a — RENT-GAP COLUMNS FOR UPCOMING (added 2026-09-19, Gabriel-approved; INFORMATIONAL ONLY — never a price recommendation, never a write). For every lease on the UPCOMING list (Lease End within 120 days, no notice), compute from the SAME workbook read as STEP 1:
