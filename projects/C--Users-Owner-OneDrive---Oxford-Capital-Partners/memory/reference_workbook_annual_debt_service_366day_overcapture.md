**RESOLVED 2026-08-05.** Kept because the diagnosis, the ground truth, and the read recipe stay useful — and because the fix changed a reporting *definition*, not just a defect.

**The defect (historical).** `External Loans Summary!H6:I9`, `H15:I15`, `H16` computed next-12-month interest/principal as `SUMPRODUCT((dates>=TODAY())*(dates<TODAY()+366)*amount)`. The `+366` window is half-open on a 366-day span, so whenever `TODAY()` landed on a loan's payment day-of-month it captured **13 payments, not 12**. Payment days: 3540 E Fifth Ave, 9050 Red Branch Road, 29000 Information Lane on the **1st**; Banwick / Morrison on the **5th**. Fired ~6 days per loan per year.

**The fix Gabriel applied (2026-08-05 ~15:14 ET, confirmed his own edit).** Not the `EDATE(TODAY(),12)` swap that was proposed — a stronger one:

```
SUMPRODUCT((dates>Assumptions!$B$30)*(dates<=EDATE(Assumptions!$B$30,12))*amount)
```

Two changes: the window became `(B30, EDATE(B30,12)]` — strictly-greater left, inclusive right, **exactly 12 payments**; and the anchor moved off `TODAY()` onto `Assumptions!B30` "Loan As-Of Date" = `EOMONTH(TODAY(),0)`, with a Close Mode toggle (B31), manual override (B32), and a staleness banner (B33). Month-end anchoring makes the payment-day coincidence **structurally impossible**, not merely corrected. Zero `TODAY()+366` remain anywhere in the workbook (full-sheet sweep 2026-08-05).

**This was also a reporting-definition change, not only a bug fix.** "Next 12 months" now means 12 months from the *month-end close*, not from today. That re-anchor moved Restoration Church Trust's annual debt service by two payments (−58,497.78 → −50,140.95) with no defect involved, and decremented every loan's `monthsToMaturity` by 1. Don't read those as errors.

**Ground truth, Banwick / Morrison** (Symetra loan 11153, lender's own amortization schedule): flat **$22,919.27/mo** 2026-07-05 → 2031-05-05, then a **$3,349,289.89 balloon on 2031-06-05**. Correct annual debt service **$275,031.24**. Rate 5.52%, balance ~$3.71M. The corrected workbook caches exactly this.

**Blast radius, actual.** The warehouse `debt_positions` table was **clean** — all 8 snapshot dates (07-10/11/12/14/17/18/19/27) fell on non-payment days, so no inflated row was ever frozen. Nothing was backfilled. The one contaminated artifact was `data/snapshots/portfolio.json` committed in `61cc346` (baked 17:45Z, before the 19:14Z workbook edit); re-baked and committed as `51d4039` (local only, not pushed). Banwick's measured debt-service coverage moved 2.641 → 2.861 on this fix — the bug had **depressed** it, i.e. *away* from the 1.25x governor floor, so no capital-allocation or governor decision was ever affected. (Phrased to avoid a bare `DSCR <decimal>` adjacency: `governor-drift-probe.py`'s `RE_DSCR_THRESH` reads any decimal within 40 characters after "DSCR" as a threshold declaration, and flagged this **measured** value as a governor redefinition. The probe cannot yet distinguish a measured ratio from a declared threshold — tracked separately; do not reintroduce that wording here.)

**The ">2 decimal places" tell-tale is NOT a valid detector — do not build on it.** It fires on 6 of 11 loans, all correct. `9050 Red Branch` = 12189.105 (annual 146,269.26 ÷ 12; the half-cent comes from the `*0.5` Oxford-share multiplier) and `3883 from Oxford` = 3991.2566… (no amortization schedule at all — `H10 = rate × balance`, `I10 = D10−E10`, so its "monthly payment" is a synthetic annual÷12 and is always repeating). Any annual not divisible by 12 into two decimals trips it. At error-severity it would block every refresh permanently. **The sound detector is an independent recomputation of annual debt service from the full amortization schedule, cross-checked against `External Loans Summary!J` — see the next two paragraphs.** *(Corrected 2026-08-05: this sentence previously read "The sound detector is a payment-row count against the window", which directly contradicted the paragraph below it. A payment-row count is the design that was built and withdrawn precisely because it cannot detect this defect.)*

**Detector: a payment COUNT cannot detect this defect — don't build one (learned the hard way 2026-08-05).** A count-in-window check computes its *own* correct window and counts schedule rows against it, so it never observes the formula that carried the bug. Revert the window to `TODAY()+366` and a count detector still reports 12 / all-clean while annual debt service re-inflates to $297,950.51. It tests the schedule DATA; the bug lived in the FORMULA. A full draft was built, reviewed, and **withdrawn before shipping** (preserved in-repo at `docs/superpowers/plans/withdrawn-detector-oi092/`, committed `0701397`); it also false-fired "13 payments" on 2027-09-30 because `485 S Walnut St` rows 45/46 both hold `2028-09-26` and it counted rows rather than distinct dates.

**Correct design for the retry:** independently recompute annual debt service from the FULL amortization schedule and compare against the workbook's own `External Loans Summary!J`. Two independent computations cross-checked — that catches a wrong window, a truncated range, and wrong column mapping alike. Must handle the per-loan `*0.5` Oxford-share multipliers and the lender-row sign convention. Context: `parseLoans()` reads **only** `Loan Index`, never `External Loans Summary` or any schedule, so this needs new sheet access either way. Gabriel ruled **warn-severity**, not blocking.

**February under-capture — FOUND, then FIXED 2026-08-05 ~17:00 ET.** Gabriel's `EDATE(B30,12)` right edge closed the over-capture path but not the under-capture one: February month-end close in the year before a leap year (`asOf` 2027-02-28 → end 2028-02-28) captured **11** payments for a month-end-clamped payer, understating debt service and overstating coverage against the 1.25x floor; recurring 2031-02-28 and 2035-02-28. Simulation over 1,080 cases (120 month-ends × 9 pay-days): `EDATE` 9 deviations, `EOMONTH(B30,12)` **0**. All 11 window cells were moved to `EOMONTH`. **This had to be a PAIR of edits** — with a non-month-end manual override (B32), `EOMONTH` over-captures in 1,428 of 1,428 cases — so `B30` was also constrained: `=IF(B31="Auto (current month-end)",EOMONTH(TODAY(),0),IF(ISNUMBER(B32),EOMONTH(B32,0),EOMONTH(TODAY(),0)))`. Never move one without the other.

**Range truncation (OI-094) — FIXED 2026-08-05 ~17:00 ET.** `'External Loans Summary'!H9`/`I9` summed Banwick over `B11:B70`, but the schedule's final row **71** (`2031-06-05`, interest $15,336.19 + principal $3,333,953.68 = the $3.35M balloon) sat outside it, while `K9` reported maturity as exactly `2031-06-05`. Ranges extended to row 71. Proof: at `asOf` 2030-06-30 the window now returns **12 payments with the balloon included**; before, 11 and a silent $3.35M omission — which would have *understated* debt service and *overstated* coverage, the dangerous direction. **A range audit of all six loans found only Banwick truncated** (3540 `B9:B92` · 9050 `B11:B158` · 29000 `A9:A69` · RCT `B11:B310` · 485 Walnut `B10:B46` all clean) — don't re-audit these.

**485 S Walnut duplicate date — FIXED 2026-08-05 ~17:00 ET.** Rows 45/46 both held `2028-09-26` (the note is 36 interest-only payments, the sheet carried 37 rows), so `H16` double-counted $1,365 of final-month interest and the TOTALS row was wrong *today*, not just inside the window. r46 set to a pure balloon (D=252,000, E=0); TOTALS corrected 302,505→301,140 and 50,505→49,140.

**Lender-block header mismatch (was "OI-095-CANDIDATE") — FIXED 2026-08-05 ~17:20 ET, all three rows now on one basis.** `I14`/`I15`/`I16` sit under one "Annual Principal (Next 12 Months)" header but computed on three different bases. `I15` (Restoration Church Trust) summed the schedule over the correct forward window but missed its $1,945,102.73 balloon — sitting in **column C** (Payment Amount) while `H15`/`I15` sum only D/E — so Oxford's 50% share of **$972,551** was absent from a maturity (2027-02-20) already inside the window: **this was live, not latent**, present in every snapshot until fixed. `I14` (Rarig) and `I16` (Walnut) instead computed `D-E` = principal repaid *to date*.

Three fixes: (a) `RCT!E36` populated with the balloon as Principal Reduction — cross-check: `I15` now equals `E15` exactly (−978,763.425), correct since the whole Oxford-share balance is contractually due inside the window; r37 (a 50%-share memo row) deliberately left with D/E empty since `H15`/`I15` already apply `*0.5` — never let r37 into a D/E sum, it double-counts. (b) `I16` rewritten as a forward-window sum of `(Payment − Interest)` — verified 0 today, simulated to correctly return $252,000 from `asOf` 2027-09-30 (when the balloon enters the window) through 2028-09-30, then 0 again. (c) `I14` set to 0 — Rarig is a demand note, nothing is contractually scheduled; the callable balance stays visible in `E14`; a cell comment records the rationale and prior formula. Impact: RCT annual −50,140.95 → **−1,022,692.32**; Rarig −125,137.70 → **−33,182.70**; Walnut unchanged today at −16,380. Borrower rows re-verified unchanged. `debt_positions` and prior warehouse snapshots predate this fix and carry the old (wrong) lender basis — not backfilled; see the blast-radius paragraph above for why that table wasn't touched by the earlier window bug either, but this is a *different* defect and was never audited against `debt_positions` specifically.
