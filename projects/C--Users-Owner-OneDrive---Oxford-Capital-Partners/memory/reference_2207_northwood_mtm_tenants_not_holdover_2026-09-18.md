Gabriel ruled 2026-09-18: **Adkins Towing** (2207 Northwood unit A8, 1,200 SF, $835/mo) and **Anibal Morales** (unit C4, 1,200 SF, $399/mo = $3.99/SF) are **month-to-month tenancies, not holdovers**.

The Master Workbook `Master Tenant Rent Roll` tab (read 2026-09-18) shows both with Lease End 2024-09-01, Status `Expired`, Occupancy Class `Holdover — expired, no notice`. That label is the workbook's mechanical classification of "past Lease End, no notice" — it does not distinguish a consensual MTM from a true holdover. Do not describe these two as holdovers, do not propose holdover-premium charges against them, and do not count them in any "holdover exposure" figure.
