# Dollar General Portfolio (New Lebanon / Columbus / Fayetteville, OH) — PASS (2026-06-08)

Matthews offering, screened via `verified-deal-recon` (3 adversarial verifiers concurred, no flips, high confidence).

**Deal:** 3 single-tenant DG net-lease RETAIL boxes, all OH, all built 2006, 3×9,014 SF. Price $2,379,882 @ 8.50% in-place cap; rent $202,290; blended $88.01/SF. NN lease (landlord owns roof/structure/lot/landscaping/HVAC>$1,000). Corporate guarantee, S&P BBB / Moody's Baa3 (downgraded 3/28/2025). Rent FLAT to expiry; 10% bumps only in options. All three leases roll within ~4 months in 2031 → ~5.1 yr WALT.

**Verdict: PASS.** Out-of-box (retail, not industrial/flex). Reasons: stacked-2031 rollover cliff (P(≥1 dark) ~60-70%); DG actively closing the marginal/rural cohort that New Lebanon + Fayetteville sit in; NN CapEx on 20-yr buildings cuts true net yield to ~7.0-8.0%; basis fair-not-protective (buys at/above dark-value floor, no margin); can't carry the LTV≤40%/DSCR≥1.25× governor at a competitive return. OM's load-bearing ~$880K Columbus dark comp could NOT be verified and was contradicted (nearest real dark DG lists $1.225M, 2013 vintage). Columbus/Lockbourne Rd has a Phase I flag (nearby Lockbourne landfill + former truss facility w/ removed USTs).

**If it recycles:** still PASS unless re-offered as industrial in the same submarkets. Broker = Matthews (worth an industrial conversation).

**Why:** Confirms ORA basis-discipline + buy-box rules reject competently-priced credit-lease retail with no value-add path.
**How to apply:** Don't re-screen this portfolio if it comes around again; reuse the cap data below for any DG/dollar-store one-off.

**DG→industrial/flex conversion precedent: essentially NONE documented** (exhaustive search). CBRE's ~14M SF retail→industrial is all BIG-box (Kmart/Sears, 50-150K SF, 20-28' clear) — does not transfer to 10K small box. Typical dark-DG reuse = other retail/service (grocer, church, daycare, urgent care). Strategy is NOVEL: no playbook (risk) / no competition (opportunity).

**Shell constraints that bite EVERY DG (not just these):** clear height ~12-14' (single-slope PEMB, low at storefront; flex wants 16-24', 14' is the floor — raising it is cost-prohibitive/permanent → caps tenant tier + rent ceiling); no dock/roll-up (cheap add ~$5-15K/door); **truck-court geometry on 1-ac front-parking lots is the systemic deal-killer**; single-phase rural power excludes fabrication. Conversion cost ~$85-220K (~$9-22/SF). All-in ~$79-122/SF; Midwest small-bay flex $6-12/SF NNN → thin spread, only works at true distressed dark buy (<$80/SF) on a physically-suitable site. Best-fit uses: contractor/trades+office, showroom-warehouse, last-mile van staging. NOT warehouse/distribution, crane mfg, or self-storage (needs ~50K SF).

**Reframed thesis takeaway:** DG-box→flex works only where a DG sits on industrially-zoned land in an industrial submarket — rare, because DG sites on retail corridors by design. Columbus/Lockbourne is exactly that rare case (M-zoned by-right, industrial context) and is worth isolating.
