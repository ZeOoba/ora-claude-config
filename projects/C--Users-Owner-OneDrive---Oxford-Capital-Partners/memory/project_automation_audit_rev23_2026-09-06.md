## ⛔ METHOD LESSON — one error produced THREE wrong answers this session

The same error produced:
1. **R23-6's wrong character counts** (1,342/1,052 raw-with-newlines vs 1,306/1,022 YAML-folded — "two over cap" was actually one), **published before being caught**;

3. a miscounted drift set (0 on a shallow walk, 7 on the correct structural walk).

**Rule: walk the structure, not the text; diff payloads, never labels.** Two registries are not duplicates until their *contents* overlap — overlapping county names and file mtimes are not evidence.
