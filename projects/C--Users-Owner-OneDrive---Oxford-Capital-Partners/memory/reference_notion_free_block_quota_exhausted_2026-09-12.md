**Finding (ora-am-brief Block B, 2026-09-12):** `notion-create-pages` against the Ops Hub parent (`32b6ecd8-9e59-81f2-b329-df3fd0e25062`) failed twice (spec-mandated retry) with:

> Your workspace has used all of its free blocks. Upgrade your plan to create more content.

This is a genuine content-storage ceiling on the Notion workspace's **free plan tier** — not a bug, not a transient error. [OI-288](open-items-tracker-history.md#OI-288) tracked it (CLOSED 2026-09-30).

**Do not confuse this with [[reference_tenant_db_cache_and_notion_quota_myth]].** That finding is about a *different* Notion limit entirely: an `entitlement_required` gate that fires only from `notion-query-data-sources` in SQL mode, and it explicitly warns "do not recommend a Notion plan upgrade for this — it fixes nothing." This new finding is the opposite case: `notion-create-pages`/block-creation genuinely needs more capacity, and — unlike that other finding — an upgrade IS the correct fix here (see the 09-18 correction below: freeing existing blocks is NOT a fix for this one, so the two findings' remedies are asymmetric, not mirror images). Two distinct Notion ceilings, two distinct remedies — don't apply one's lesson to the other.

**Why to apply:** any task that calls `notion-create-pages` or `notion-create-database` (new content) will now fail identically until this is resolved — `ora-am-brief` Blocks B/C/D, `ora-pm-ledger`, `automation-health-monitor`, dashboard refreshes, etc. `notion-update-page`, `notion-search`, and `notion-fetch` (reads and edits to EXISTING pages) are unaffected — confirmed live: this session's `notion-fetch`/`notion-search` calls against existing pages worked fine throughout, only the create call failed.

**Root cause + pricing (web-verified 2026-09-18, verbatim from notion.com/pricing + notion.com/help/understanding-block-usage, cross-checked against a third-party discussion):** Notion's free plan caps workspaces at **1,000 blocks once the workspace has 2+ members** (solo free workspaces are unlimited — this cap only bites multi-member workspaces, which matches ORA's). At the cap: existing content stays fully readable/editable (matches the confirmed update-still-works behavior), only *new* block/page/database creation is refused.

**CORRECTION 2026-09-18 — deleting/archiving does NOT free blocks, at all, ever.** The 09-12/09-14 "fix direction (b)" above and this file's own earlier cost/benefit paragraph were WRONG and must not be acted on. Verbatim from Notion's official help center (`notion.com/help/understanding-block-usage`, fetched via browser and read as raw page text, not paraphrased): *"We count blocks based on the amount of content created in your workspace since signing up — deleting blocks, or emptying the trash, will not reduce your block count."* And its own FAQ, verbatim: *"Can I delete blocks to avoid these restrictions? No, deleting content does not reduce your block count or free up additional blocks. Once your workspace has created 1,000 blocks, it will experience restrictions — regardless of subsequent deletions."* The count is **cumulative blocks ever created since signup**, not current live content — so archiving the tagged `🗑️ [DUPLICATE — SUPERSEDED]` pages, or anything else, does nothing, not even temporarily. **The ONLY fix is upgrading to a paid plan.** Plus plan ($10/seat/month billed monthly, ~20% less annual) removes the block cap entirely ("unlimited blocks") — Business ($20/seat/month) also removes it but adds features ORA doesn't need for this problem.

Exact current block count is NOT visible via the Notion MCP connector — plan-tier/block-usage is a Settings → Upgrade Plan UI figure, not an API-exposed one. **Seat count now confirmed 2026-09-18 via `notion-get-users`: exactly 2 human members — Gabriel Goldberg and Eric Goldberg (everything else in the workspace is a bot/integration: Composio, Cowork Heartbeat Reader, Notion MCP, ORA Command Center, ORA Owner Outreach Writer, ORA Power BI, ORA-Zapier-API, Qodo, Zapier — none of these are billable seats).** This confirms the "2+ workspace owners" free-plan trigger. At 2 seats, Plus plan pricing is ~$20/month (2 × $10/seat monthly) or ~$16/month equivalent on annual billing (2 × ~$8/seat, ~20% discount) — confirm exact annual rate in Notion's checkout, this is the advertised discount rate, not a quoted invoice figure.
