**Standing procurement stance (Gabriel-approved 2026-06-09):** While ORA is below ~$150M AUM (currently ~$60M), the marginal tooling dollar goes to **compounding the Claude/agent-infra layer** (skills, scheduled tasks, dynamic workflows — near-zero marginal cost, no migration risk, ORA owns the IP) rather than **per-seat third-party PropTech SaaS** (Cactus, Primer, Dealpath, VTS, Prophia, MRI, Yardi Voyager/Virtuoso enterprise) that needs institutional AUM to amortize.

**Why:** Validated repeatedly by the weekly tooling-intel sweep — the highest-value *new* tooling keeps landing in ORA's own agent layer, not third-party platforms. The AUM-gate auto-parks platform-migration plays until $150M; their weekly re-surfacing is the gate working, not a buy signal. Per-seat SaaS ROI is negative at sub-$150M scale.

**How to apply:**
- In [[reference_tech]]/tooling decisions and the `ora-weekly-tooling-intel-sweep`, score against this filter: prefer plug-in/workflow tools that extend Notion/HubSpot/QB/Outlook over migrations.
- Bridges that deepen the existing stack (e.g. HubSpot Breeze MCP → Notion) are in-bounds; replacements are not.
- Hold the AUM gate — do not re-litigate parked platform-migration tools each time they re-surface.
- Revisit the whole stance at $150M AUM.
